## 2. Role Profiles

### A. Sales Officer (SO)
- **HR Designations (live):** Sales Officer, Senior Sales Officer, Junior Sales Officer, Territory Sales Officer
- **Reports to:** Zonal Officer
- **Span of control:** ~437 points / 628 sections nationwide
- **Core responsibility:** Own the point/section — daily sales execution against memo target.
- **Key responsibilities:**
  - Achieve monthly memo/BDT sales target for assigned points.
  - Daily outlet visit & order capture (RTM beat route).
  - New outlet enrolment and outlet master accuracy.
  - Distributor coordination — stock, delivery, and credit/collection follow-up.
  - Price & scheme communication; merchandising/visibility at retail.
- **KPIs:**
  - Sales vs target (memo & BDT).
  - Outlets visited / day and coverage %.
  - New outlet additions.
  - Collection & no-outstanding days.
  - Strike rate (orders ÷ visits).
- **Authority:** Field-level pricing within approved scheme; no credit beyond distributor limit.

### B. Zonal Officer (Zonal Sales Manager)
- **HR Designations (live):** Zonal Sales Manager, Area Sales Manager, Territory Manager
- **Reports to:** DSM
- **Span of control:** 1 Zone (64 zones nationwide)
- **Core responsibility:** Drive zone sales, manage SOs, and distributor performance.
- **Key responsibilities:**
  - Own zone target & achievement; gap analysis and corrective action.
  - Supervise & coach Sales Officers (daily ride-along, route review).
  - Distributor management — fill rates, stock norms, primary vs secondary sales.
  - Resolve territory-level issues (market, outlet, competitor).
- **KPIs:**
  - Zone sales vs target.
  - SO productivity (sales per officer, strike rate).
  - Distributor fill rate & stock freshness.
  - Zone outlet coverage & new-outlet growth.
- **Authority:** Approve local trade schemes within budget; recommend SO transfers/rewards.

### C. DSM (Divisional Sales Manager)
- **HR Designations (live):** Divisional Sales Manager, Assistant Divisional Sales Manager
- **Reports to:** NSM
- **Span of control:** 1 Region (6 regions nationwide)
- **Core responsibility:** Regional P&L, sales strategy execution, team leadership.
- **Key responsibilities:**
  - Own regional sales target & market share.
  - Manage Zonal Officers; set zone-wise targets.
  - Distributor & channel strategy for the region.
  - Monitor secondary sales, pricing, and competition.
  - Demand planning coordination with supply chain.
- **KPIs:**
  - Region sales vs target & growth.
  - Market share and distribution reach.
  - Zone performance variance.
  - Distributor ROI & engagement.
- **Authority:** Approve regional trade spend; final sign-off on zone targets within NSM guidelines.

### D. NSM (National Sales Manager)
- **HR Designations (live):** National Sales Manager
- **Reports to:** Head of Sales / Chief Sales Officer
- **Span of control:** National (Consumer)
- **Core responsibility:** National sales strategy, top-line delivery, and channel policy.
- **Key responsibilities:**
  - Set national sales targets and cascade to regions/zones.
  - Own national P&L (revenue, trade spend, gross margin).
  - Channel strategy — distributor model, modern trade, coverage.
  - Team development & succession across the sales force.
  - Pricing, trade scheme, and promotion policy approval.
- **KPIs:**
  - National sales vs target & growth.
  - Market share & distribution metrics.
  - Trade spend as % of sales.
  - Sales force productivity & attrition.
- **Authority:** Approve national schemes & pricing within budget; propose budget to Head of Sales.

### E. Head of Sales
- **HR Designations (live):** Head of Sales, Chief Sales Officer
- **Reports to:** CEO / Managing Director
- **Span of control:** All channels (Consumer + Bulk)
- **Core responsibility:** Full revenue ownership and sales organization leadership.
- **Key responsibilities:**
  - Define sales vision, strategy, and annual business plan.
  - Own total revenue, market share, and profitability.
  - Lead NSM/DSM leadership team; talent & capability building.
  - Channel expansion & new market entry decisions.
  - Cross-functional alignment (marketing, supply chain, finance).
- **KPIs:**
  - Total revenue & growth.
  - Market share & category leadership.
  - Gross margin & trade-spend efficiency.
  - Organization health (attrition, capability).
- **Authority:** Full sales budget & organizational authority; strategic pricing/policy decisions.
